\chapter*{Introduction}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Introduction}

This course assumes mastery of A-Level Mathematics (and Further Mathematics), in particular differentiation, integration and associated techniques, curve sketching, and elementary mechanics. 
Ultimately, this is a methods-based course, and while a certain level of mathematical communication and clarity is expected, we do not focus on rigorously proving facts about calculus in this course (that is the purpose of MATH40002, Analysis I).
